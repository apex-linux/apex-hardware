## What the failures mean

*   **display:** the owner chose scale 1.5 on Ubuntu. The panel runs
    2880x1800 at 90 Hz and brightness works. Apex's default for this panel
    is scale 2, which `apex-desktop` sets; this row passes once it does.
*   **battery:** the limit does not work on Ubuntu at all. `asus-nb-wmi`
    exposes only `charge_control_end_threshold`, and UPower 1.91.1's
    `60-upower-battery.rules` imports its `CHARGE_LIMIT` only when
    `charge_control_start_threshold` exists. UPower therefore reports
    `ChargeThresholdSupported` false, GNOME Settings shows no charge limit
    option, and the battery charges to 100%. The battery has done 751
    cycles and holds 61% of its design capacity.

## What this run does not show

*   **cpu, wifi:** no suspend happened in this boot, so resume is untested.
*   **audio:** the pins and PipeWire routes are correct and the kernel
    applies no quirk, but nobody listened. The listening test in
    [acceptance.md](../acceptance.md) is still to do.
*   **fingerprint:** fprintd sees the reader through libfprint's `elan`
    driver, but no finger is enrolled, so enrollment on this build is
    unproven.
*   **dgpu:** the GPU sits in D3cold and was suspended 93% of the time since
    boot. Offload to it was not exercised.
